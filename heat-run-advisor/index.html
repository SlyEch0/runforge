WBGT is a stronger predictor of heat stress for outdoor exercise than Heat Index. UV is independent of temperature. Recommended pace adjusts your target for current conditions.
Heat Index (NWS, °F)
< 80°F — Caution (fatigue possible)
80–89°F — Caution
90–102°F — Extreme Caution
103–124°F — Danger
≥ 125°F — Extreme Danger

Feels-like temperature in the shade. Full sun can add up to ~15°F. Prolonged activity raises risk of cramps, exhaustion, or stroke.

WBGT (ACSM athletic guidelines, °C)
< 18°C — Low / Green Flag
18–22.9°C — Moderate / Yellow
23–27.9°C — High / Red Flag
28–32.2°C — Very High / limit intensity
≥ 32.3°C — Extreme / cancel outdoor hard efforts

Best single metric for outdoor exercise. Combines temperature, humidity, and (in true instruments) solar load & wind. Our value is an approximation from air temp + humidity only.

UV Index (WHO / EPA)
0–2 — Low
3–5 — Moderate
6–7 — High
8–10 — Very High
11+ — Extreme

Independent of heat. High UV causes sunburn and long-term skin damage even on cooler days. Use sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses at 6+.
